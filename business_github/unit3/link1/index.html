Unit 3 · Self-study: Business etiquette

Before class: learn 25 workplace words and phrases, read three short texts about business etiquette, then think, comment and solve three real workplace cases. No recording needed.

How to study (60–75 minutes) · Cách tự học
  • ① Vocabulary: learn 25 words and phrases, then check yourself. Học 25 từ/cụm rồi tự kiểm tra.
  • ② Reading: three texts on business etiquette. Target words are highlighted. 3 bài đọc, từ mục tiêu được tô vàng.
  • ③ Case study: brainstorm → give your comment → suggest a solution. Nghĩ ý, nhận xét, đưa giải pháp cho tình huống thật.
  • The progress bar shows how much you have done. Finish with 📨 Submit.
