Unit 4 · Self-study: Creating a buzz

Before class: learn 25 words and phrases about brands and products, read three short texts about brand image and marketing, then think, comment and solve three real workplace cases. No recording needed.

How to study (60–75 minutes) · Cách tự học
  • ① Vocabulary: learn 25 words and phrases, then check yourself. Học 25 từ/cụm rồi tự kiểm tra.
  • ② Reading: three texts about brands, describing products and promotion. Target words are highlighted. 3 bài đọc, từ mục tiêu được tô vàng.
  • ③ Case study: brainstorm → give your comment → suggest a solution. Nghĩ ý, nhận xét, đưa giải pháp cho tình huống thật.
  • The progress bar shows how much you have done. Finish with 📨 Submit.
